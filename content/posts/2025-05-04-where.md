In October of 2021 Wake County Board of Commissioners voted to adopt a [non-descrimination ordinance (NDO)](https://s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/wakegov.com.if-us-west-1/s3fs-public/documents/2021-10/NDOFinalDraft10-14-21.pdf).  It benefits white cis women the most.  It was written to aid the most marginalized folks of Wake County (trans women of marginalized race and economic status).  It also has no effect in municipalities that did not adopt it[^1].
